**Summary:**

<!-- SUMMARY:model:x_mr_config -->
MR config is a Material Request line: a requested product and quantity on a Material Request, with quantities transferred, quantity to transfer, transfer remainder and on-hand quantity in the request's warehouse. This repo computes the transfer remainder, the on-hand quantity and the quantity still available to transfer. The automations that restrict creating, deleting and archiving lines and update and validate quantities are in BugFix-Purchase.
